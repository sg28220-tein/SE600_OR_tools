Acceptance meter — should we accept x′?

Left: the chance of accepting a worse move, P = e−δ/T, for every size of worsening δ. The dashed curve is at the start (T0), the solid one at the current T. Right: the random number r is drawn on the 0–1 bar. Below P (green) → accept; above P (red) → reject.

Thermometer — the cooling schedule

T is multiplied by α after every iters_per_T moves, until it falls below T_min. What "hot" means depends on δ (slide 15). The bands use a typical worsening δ: HOT = it is accepted more than half the time, WARM = sometimes, COLD = less than 2 times in 100.

Fast cooling (α = 0.90) is a quench: few temperature levels, so the search freezes in the first valley it falls into. Slow cooling spends many more evaluations and usually ends lower. Press the button to see it.
